START IN YOUR TERMINAL

Your data stays yours.
Your chart goes anywhere.

cctrack reads usage already stored on your computer. See your activity in the terminal or export a shareable SVG—no account, cloud sync, or API key needed for local Claude Code tracking.

● ● ●TERMINAL
$ pip install cctrack-ai
$ cctrack heatmap
$ cctrack heatmap --metric active-days
$ cctrack heatmap --output my-ai-year.svg
See install instructions ↗

SMALL TOOL. GOOD SIGNAL.

A habit you can actually see.

01

Collect locally

cctrack reads supported AI usage from your machine and builds a private history. No prompt contents are needed.

02

See your rhythm

One glance shows when you were building with AI—across days, weeks, and the year.

03

Share your year

Export a clean SVG for your README, profile, or social post. Keep the details you want private.

⌂

LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Your sessions stay on your machine.

Local tracking doesn’t upload transcripts or project names. The heatmap export contains only the dates, metric, and activity levels needed to draw your chart. Public profiles and private team boards are future ideas—not features that are live today.
